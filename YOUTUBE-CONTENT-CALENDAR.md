## Production checklist (repeat for each video)
1. Write full narration script (~1,500 words / ~9-10 min read)
2. Draft ~18-20 key-scene storyboard with locked style formula
3. Generate stills (gpt_image_2_5, retry with simpler wording if content-flagged)
4. Animate each still (kling3_0, sound off)
5. Upscale all clips to 4K (Topaz)
6. Generate matching AI voiceover per clip (text2speech_v2, same voice for channel consistency)
7. Assemble in editor, add background music bed (-18 to -24 dB under narration)
8. Export, publish, then cut 4-6 Shorts from the finished clips over the following 2 weeks
