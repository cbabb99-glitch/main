Before you order

Confirm three things with the State Fire Marshal's office first. The size and color rules in R710-1-7 weren't readable in the copies I found, so these are placeholders:
  • The required tag size and color (this draft uses the common 2.75 × 5.25 in; the condemned tag must be red and at least as large as your service tag).
  • The exact Seal of Registration artwork and your license type wording. Both come with your license.
  • What "Provisions of Section 6.7" means for your tag. The rule lists it as required information.
Ordering tips
  • Ask an extinguisher tag printer for a proof matching this layout. They know state tag formats and print on durable stock with a reinforced hole.
  • Front holds only the required items, as the rule requires. Anything else, such as your phone and "next due" lines, stays on the back.
  • A new tag goes on each extinguisher every time you service it. A brand-new unit with a printed manufacture date doesn't need a tag until one year after that date.
  • Order matching verification-of-service collars printed with your company name for recharges.
  • Your 6-year and hydro subcontractor tags their own work, so you mostly use Annual, Recharge, Repair and New installation.
